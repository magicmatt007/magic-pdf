## What's new in 0.12.0

**A tidier window**
- The search box now sits at the right end of the row of open files, so the toolbar
  fits on one row.
- The sidebar's tabs (Pages, Outline, Comments, Search) stay on one line. When their
  names don't fit, they show icons, with the name of the open tab next to its icon.
- Dialogs and the update notice have more room around their contents.
- The current page in the page overview has a thinner orange frame and a page number
  that is easier to read in the dark look.

**Comments**
- Long comments in the sidebar are shortened to a few lines. Click a comment to read
  all of it.

**Updates**
- The update notice's main button stands out, and its first line is now in German too.
