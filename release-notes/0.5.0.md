## What's new in 0.5.0

**Deutsch**
- The interface is now also in German: **Edit → Settings → Language** (System, English, Deutsch).

**Compare and measure**
- **File → Compare with** shows two PDFs side by side, scrolling and zooming together. Words only on the left are marked red, words only on the right green. A bar steps through the pages with changes.
- **Distance and area measuring** (in the shapes menu, or M) uses a drawing scale (1:n, mm to ft). The result is drawn on the page and kept as the comment.

**Pages**
- **Pages → Crop pages** trims the margins. Undo brings them back.
- **Pages → Header and footer** adds texts with page numbers, `{date}` and `{file}`. Applying it again replaces them, and they can be removed again.
- **Bookmarks** can be edited in the Outline tab: add, rename, move, indent and delete.
- **File → Save optimized copy** writes a smaller file and can scale down large pictures.

**Search and viewing**
- Search results show the text around each match, and the search can cover all open tabs (search options menu).
- Pages stay sharp at high zoom.
- The toolbar is tidier: page layouts and search options are each in one menu.
