## What's new in 0.11.6

**Easier to see**
- Empty text fields, such as the author name in Settings, can now be seen in both the
  light and the dark look. Before, they blended into the window.
- In the dark look, buttons and empty checkboxes in windows stand out more clearly.

**Keyboard**
- The list of keyboard shortcuts now includes Ctrl+Q (quit) and Ctrl+Shift+Z (redo).
