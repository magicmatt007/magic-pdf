## What's new in 0.16.0

**Edit text**
- While you type, the page shows the change right away, in the PDF's own font.
- Click a line to edit its whole paragraph. The text wraps to the paragraph's width,
  lines you did not change stay as they were, and Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- Drag text to move it, or change its size and colour in the edit panel.
- Delete text with the bin button in the edit panel.

**Find and replace**
- Edit → Replace text (Ctrl+H) replaces a word or phrase on all pages at once and tells
  you how many places were changed. Undo brings everything back in one step.
