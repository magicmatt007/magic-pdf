## What's new in 0.6.0

**E-invoices (ZUGFeRD / Factur-X)**
- PDFs with embedded invoice data get a bar above the page and an **E-invoice** window. It shows seller and buyer, invoice details, items, VAT, totals and payment details, and **Save XML…** exports the data.
- magic-pdf checks that the amounts add up, that the amount due appears on the page, and that the fields EN 16931 requires are present (e.g. the payment means).

**Attachments**
- A new **Attachments** tab in the sidebar lists files embedded in a PDF. You can open or save them.

**Document properties**
- **File → Document properties** (Ctrl+D) edits title, author, subject and keywords. It also shows the program the PDF was created with, dates, PDF version, page and file size. The changes also update the XMP metadata, so PDF/A files stay valid.
