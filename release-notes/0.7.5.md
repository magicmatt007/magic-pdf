## What's new in 0.7.5

**Updates**
- The update window shows what's new in the release, neatly formatted, instead of the
  full release page text with the download instructions.
- Linux: a copy installed from source with `install.sh` now installs updates itself.
  Click **Install**: magic-pdf downloads and checks the new version, replaces the
  installed one and starts again. Your menu entry and "Open with" stay as they are.
