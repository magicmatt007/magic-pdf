## What's new in 0.8.0

**A new look**
- magic-pdf has its own colours now: warm, light backgrounds instead of plain grey,
  and a matching warm dark mode.
- What is switched on or selected — the active tool, the open tab, the current page —
  is marked in the orange of the magic-pdf icon.
- Rounder buttons and windows, the app icon at the start of the menu bar, and an orange
  **Install** button when an update is ready.
