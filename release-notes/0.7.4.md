## What's new in 0.7.4

This release collects the fixes of 0.7.2 and 0.7.3, whose release pages had no
description.

**Tabs**
- On Windows, dragging a tab out of the window works: let go outside the window to open
  the document in its own window, or over another magic-pdf window to move it there.
  (Before, a dragged tab could only be moved within the tab bar.)
- Dragging a tab well down onto the page also opens it in its own window, like in web
  browsers. This works in a maximized window too.

**Text boxes**
- Text boxes now use the same colours as Adobe Acrobat and PDF-XChange: boxes made there
  keep their fill, border and text colours when you move or edit them in magic-pdf, and
  magic-pdf's boxes look right in those programs. Boxes saved by older magic-pdf versions
  are converted the next time you move or edit them.
