## What's new in 0.10.0

**Sticky notes in your colours**
- Sticky notes now show the colour you picked instead of always being yellow. Notes
  you already saved change to their colour the next time you recolour or move them.

**Comments**
- Drag a sticky note, shape, highlight or any other annotation onto another page to
  move it there, together with its comment and replies. One undo puts it back.
- The Comments sidebar has a new **With comment** filter: it lists only annotations
  someone wrote a comment on or replied to.

**Highlighting**
- Dragging back to where you started now selects nothing, so no highlight is added.
  Selections also start exactly where you pressed the mouse button.
