## What's new in 0.11.4

**Comments and text boxes**
- A comment or text box text you are still typing is now included when you save,
  print or close. Before, pressing Ctrl+S or clicking Save right after typing could
  save without it, and closing the window did not ask to save it.

**Drawings**
- Moving, resizing or undoing a freehand drawing no longer makes its frame grow a
  little each time; the frame stays exactly where you drag it.

**Review status**
- The review status of a comment (Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled, Completed) now
  appears in other PDF applications such as PDF-XChange and Acrobat once you save.
