## What's new in 0.13.0

**Digital signing**
- Sign PDFs with a certificate: choose File → Sign with certificate (or the button in
  the Signature tool's options), drag a box on the page and pick your certificate.
- On Windows, use a certificate from the Windows certificate store, including smart
  cards (Windows asks for the PIN). Everywhere, a certificate file (.p12 or .pfx) works
  too; its password is asked once per session.
- The signature box shows your saved signature picture, your name and the date, and the
  reason and location if you enter them.
- A trusted timestamp can be added, so the signature can still be checked after your
  certificate expires (needs an internet connection).
- The signature dialog shows who the certificate names and until when it is valid, and
  warns when a certificate is self-made.

**Signed documents**
- Adding comments to a signed PDF and saving it no longer breaks its signatures: the
  changes are added after the signed part, and the signature shows "changed after
  signing".
