## What's new in 0.3.0

**macOS**
- magic-pdf now runs on macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel): a `.dmg` to drag into Applications. PDFs opened from Finder join the running window as tabs, and shortcuts are shown with ⌘/⇧/⌥.
- Updates on macOS: **Install** downloads and opens the new disk image.

**Thumbnail sidebar**
- Thumbnails fill the sidebar width; a **Size** slider and Ctrl+wheel over the thumbnails make them bigger or smaller, and a wide sidebar shows several columns.
- Rows are as tall as their pages, so landscape pages no longer leave gaps.
- The current page is easier to spot (stronger border, page-number badge).
- A sidebar button at the left of the toolbar shows and hides it (also F9). The sidebar can be made much narrower, and its width and the thumbnail size are remembered.

**Fixes**
- New text boxes use the text colour and size chosen in the tool options bar.
- Linux: the window title shows in the title bar on GNOME/Wayland.
