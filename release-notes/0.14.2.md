## What's new in 0.14.2

**Signing on Windows**
- The list of certificates from the Windows certificate store now shows only those you
  can sign with: certificates that have expired or are meant for something else, such
  as logging in to your work account, are left out. "Show all certificates" lists them
  too.
- When the chosen certificate has expired or is not meant for signing, the signing
  window says so.
